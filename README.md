# Join Lumenforge AI

Low threshold. No resume, no sponsor, no minimum stars.

## Who can join

Anyone with a GitHub account who wants to follow or contribute to AI experiments, notes, or tools. You do not need prior contributions.

## How to request

1. Open a new issue in this repository: [New join request](https://github.com/Lumenforge-AI/join/issues/new?template=join.yml).
2. Title: `Join request: your-username`
3. Include:
   - GitHub username
   - One or two sentences on why you want to join
   - Optional: a project, interest, or link
4. An owner reviews it. Genuine short requests are usually invited within a day.
5. Accept the organization invitation from your email or [organization invitations](https://github.com/settings/organizations).
6. Make your membership public: [People](https://github.com/orgs/Lumenforge-AI/people) → find yourself → Private → Public.

## After you join

- Star this repo if you want the org easier to find
- Read [lab](https://github.com/Lumenforge-AI/lab) for current experiments
- Open issues or PRs when you have something to add

## What we do not do

- No paid membership
- No automatic invite bots (owners send the invitation)
- No spam or unrelated promotion in the join issue
